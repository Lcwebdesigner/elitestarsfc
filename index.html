Elite Stars Football Club

Established in 2024 in Bunyala West, Navakholo, Kakamega County, Elite Stars FC was created with one clear purpose: to provide a home for talent that might otherwise remain undiscovered.

We believe football can identify potential, develop discipline and create opportunities for players to grow both on and off the pitch.

#WeAreWe

Identifying. Nurturing. Developing. Building the next generation of football talent.
