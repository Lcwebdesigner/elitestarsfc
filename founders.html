LEADERSHIP

Our Founders

Elite Stars FC was built on vision, discipline and a shared belief in the power of football to change lives. Meet the two young leaders behind the club.

LC

Luswa Clinton

Co-Founder — IT Specialist & Web Developer

A sharp, self-driven technologist, Luswa Clinton brings together web design and software development with a passion for teaching — mentoring the next generation of programmers as a C programming tutor. His technical vision has been instrumental in building and maintaining Elite Stars FC's digital presence.

CW

Collins Wayne

Co-Founder — Criminologist & Advocate

Collins Wayne combines a background in criminology with his practice as a legal advocate, bringing discipline, integrity and a deep understanding of justice to the club's leadership. His commitment to community and accountability mirrors Elite Stars FC's mission to develop character both on and off the pitch.
